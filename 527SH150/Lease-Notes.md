# Lease notes: what to ask Suzie for

*Draft received Sept 27, 2026. Assessment Sept 28. Suzie invited edits: "Anything you can think of to improve call or text."*

The draft is minimal and mostly favorable: rent includes utilities, pets and guests allowed, grace to the 10th, $200 deposit, late fee well under the NM 10% cap. The gaps are things left undefined, and undefined resolves in the landlord's favor when there's a disagreement. Not legal advice.

## Ask for these (in order)

1. **Start and end dates.** "One year" from nothing. Move-in date and end date in writing, converting to month-to-month on the same terms after.
2. **Early termination.** "Unless terminated prior" has no notice period. Ask for a mutual right to end with 30 days written notice. On a one-year lease this protects me more than her.
3. **Owner or subletter?** Confirm Suzie owns the house. If she rents, her lease may forbid subletting.
4. **What exactly I'm renting.** Which bedroom, private or shared bath, closet, storage, kitchen, laundry, yard, garage, **a plowed parking spot** (Hwy 150 is the ski valley road).
5. **Define "utilities included."** Electric, heat (wood/propane: who splits, who fills, is it in the $800), water, trash, **internet** (Kit Carson fiber or Starlink; my income depends on it). Any cap.
6. **Last month's rent.** The "paid on" line is blank. Confirm whether she expects $800 up front, and that it applies to the final month.
7. **Entry to my room.** A lock on the door; 24 hours notice except emergencies (NM default).
8. **No rent increase during the term.**
9. **Move-in condition.** Walkthrough with photos, attached. Note furnished or not.
10. **Name Granite** in the pets line so it can't be revoked later. Ask whether he can be home alone on ride days.
11. **Fill or strike the blanks.** Quiet hours; strike the alcohol time window entirely.

## Additional Terms to propose

- Studio corner: permission for a small workbench in a shed or garage corner and a ceiling hook in my room for mobiles.
- A 2x4 mushroom grow tent, vented, in the shed or my room.
- A monthly 20-minute house check-in.
- Overnight-guest heads-up: a text the day before.

## Minor

- Rent payment method and receipts; who gets a copy of the signed lease.
- What happens if she sells or moves out during the term.
- Use of the address for residency, driver's license, mail (almost certainly yes).

## For me, not for the lease

- **Mold check before moving in.** I react hard. Bathroom, under sinks, roof line, any musty smell.
- **Alcohol in the house.** The lease allows it. Ask how much is around and whether the kitchen can be a low-alcohol zone. One plain conversation, early. Avoiding hard conversations with landlords is a pattern I've named in therapy; this is the first rep.
