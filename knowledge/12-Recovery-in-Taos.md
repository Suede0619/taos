## The 90-in-90 for a new town

1. **Week 1:** Turn It Over Early every morning it fits. Wednesday Red Willow in El Prado. Thursday men's meeting. Introduce myself as new in town every time.
2. **Week 2-4:** pick a home group. Take a service position (coffee, greeter, literature). Ask a man I respect to be a temporary sponsor.
3. **Month 2-3:** new 4th step (planned in 2025). Offer to sponsor.
4. **Ongoing:** an Acudetox clinic at a Taos recovery program would make my service and my profession the same thing. See [[15-Acudetox-in-New-Mexico]].

## Alcohol and the house

The lease allows drinking in the house and lists no hours. Suzie may drink; that's her home. Ask before moving in how much alcohol is around and whether the kitchen can be a low-alcohol zone. Say it plainly, once, early. Avoiding hard conversations with landlords is a pattern I've named in therapy.

## Alcohol-centric events to skip or attend with care

Brewski (Jan 9), Taos Winter Wine Festival (Feb 4-6), the après scene at the Bavarian and the Martini Tree. The mountain, the temple, the sangha, the dance floor and the pottery studio are all alcohol-free by default. The knowledge base is built around them on purpose.
