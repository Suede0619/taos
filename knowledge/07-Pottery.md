# Pottery: the studio down the road, and the clay scene

*Researched 2026-09-28.*

## Logan Wannamaker Pottery / Arroyo Seco Gallery, 482a NM-150

About 200 yards from the house. This is almost certainly "the pottery studio just down the road."

- Phone 575-770-4334, potmaker@gmail.com, https://www.loganwannamaker.com/
- Logan moved to Taos in 2006 and formerly directed Taos Clay.
- **Kilns:** a 20-foot-plus **anagama wood kiln**, fired about twice a year (3 days to load, ~5 days to fire, 6-7 cords of pine, cottonwood and elm), plus a 25 cu ft gas soda kiln built 2022. **Each firing takes a crew of 10-15 volunteers.** His own words: "it takes a community to fire many of the kilns we have built around Taos." https://www.loganwannamaker.com/kilns
- Classes: wheel-throwing, no experience needed, max 4 students, per an older listing. The class page currently 404s, so winter classes are **uncertain**. An apprenticeship program is also mentioned (page 404).
- Second gallery: Ojo Gallery, 11 NM-414, Ojo Caliente (three resident ceramicists; welcomes workshop proposals). Combine with an Ojo soak day.
- Gallery hours not posted; call ahead.

**The ask, week one:** walk down, introduce myself as the new neighbor at 527, say I want to learn the wheel and I want to be on the next anagama firing crew. Firing a wood kiln for five days in shifts with a dozen people is exactly the "holding each other's lives" partnership I loved in climbing, translated to clay. It is also the fastest way into Seco's social fabric.

## Rottenstone Pottery, 486 NM-150 (next door)

Scott Rutherford, wood-fired functional ware. Daily 10-5, seasonal variation. (575) 770-6183, rottenstoneseco@gmail.com, https://rottenstonegallery.com/ . No classes advertised. Say hello anyway; two wood-fire potters on one block is a scene.

## Seco Potter Fest

Fifth annual was Sept 5-7, 2026 at the Arroyo Seco Live stage: ceramic artists, demos, music. Plan to be part of the 2027 one. https://www.secolive.org/events

## Taos Ceramics Center, 114 Este Es Rd, Taos (20 min)

- 575-758-2580, info@taosceramics.com, https://www.taosceramics.com/
- Founded 2020 by Jules and Georgia Epstein to replace Taos Clay. Max 8 per class; every class includes open studio time.
- Fall 2026 (mostly sold out): Beginning Wheel Throwing (Lee Akins, Sat 10-1, 8 weeks, $385 plus clay), Intro to Clay ($365), Raku/Saggar ($395), 4-week glazing intensives ($225).
- **FlexPass**, annual studio membership, and private studio rental exist; prices not online. **Email now for the winter/spring 2027 schedule and FlexPass pricing.** Winter classes probably start January.

## UNM-Taos ceramics (Klauer Campus, Ranchos de Taos)

- Fall 2026: Intro to Ceramics ARTS 1310 (Megan Bartley-Matthews, Tue/Fri 9:00-2:30), Ceramics I ARTS 1320 (Thu 9:00-2:30). Also Sculpture I (Wed), Jewelry & Small Metals I (Fri), Drawing, Painting, Printmaking.
- **Spring 2027 schedule** publishes in October; semester starts mid-January. Non-degree students register in person at Padre Martinez Hall. NM resident community-college rates.
- Art dept (575) 737-3698, taosarts@unm.edu, https://www.unmtaosart.com/ and https://taos.unm.edu/academics/classes/class-schedule.html
- A Tue/Fri 9-2:30 class would collide with qi gong and weekday riding. The Thursday Ceramics I section fits better.
