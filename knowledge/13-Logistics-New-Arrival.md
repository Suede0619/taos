# Logistics: arriving in Arroyo Seco

*Researched 2026-09-28.*

## The house: 527 State Route 150, Arroyo Seco, NM 87514

Lease with Susan (Suzie) Nelson, $800/month including utilities, one year, deposit $200 paid 9/26/2026. Full draft and my asks: `../527 HWY 150/Room Rental Agreement - Draft.md` and `../527 HWY 150/Lease-Notes.md`.

**Before signing, from the knowledge base:**
- Confirm start and end dates, a mutual 30-day early-out, what "utilities" covers (heat source and internet especially), a lock on my door and 24-hour entry notice, and no rent increase in the term.
- **Granite:** name him in the lease. Ask about being home alone on ride days.
- **Mold:** test the house before moving in (I react hard). A cheap ERMI or just a careful look at the bathroom, under sinks, and the roof line.
- **Alcohol in the house:** the lease allows it. Ask how much is around; one plain conversation, early.
- **Studio corner and grow tent:** ask for a shed or garage corner for a workbench and a 2x4 mushroom tent. Put it in Additional Terms.
- **Standing check-in:** a monthly 20-minute coffee with Suzie about how the house is going. This is the anti-abandonment device. Nothing accumulates.
- **Internet:** Kit Carson fiber reaches much of Taos County; otherwise Starlink. Ask what's there. My income depends on it.
- **Heat:** rural homes here run wood plus propane. Ask who splits wood, who fills the tank, and whether it's in the $800.

## Government and admin

## Getting around

- **NCRTD Blue Bus 341 TSV Green**: fare-free, stops in Arroyo Seco, ski season only (last year Dec 20 to Mar 22, Wed-Sun). https://www.ncrtd.org/all-routes/341-tsv-green/ , 866-206-0754. No year-round Seco route.
- Winter tires below 45°F even with AWD. Hwy 150 above Seco is plowed but icy mornings. The Bavarian road needs 4x4. Road conditions: nmroads.com or 511.
- Santa Fe 75 min via NM-68 through the gorge (beautiful, icy in shade). Albuquerque 2.5 hr. Denver 5 hr.

## Altitude and winter

Seco ~7,600 ft, TSV base 9,200, Kachina 12,481. Boulder was 5,400. Two easy weeks before hard riding. Water constantly. Osha tea. Sunscreen at altitude even in January. Wood heat dries the air: a humidifier in the bedroom helps the sinuses and the mushrooms don't mind either.
