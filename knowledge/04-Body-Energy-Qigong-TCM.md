# Body, energy, qi gong, Chinese medicine, and contemplative practice

*Researched 2026-09-28. "verified" = fetched from the source page; "uncertain" = from snippets or older listings, confirm before relying on it.*

The frame: my body has a knee, a hip and a spine that need daily care, and my stated goal is energy awareness. Qi gong three mornings a week is the physical therapy, the meditation, and the community, all at once. See [[01-Me-in-Taos]] for the medical picture.

- https://richardleirer.org/qigong-academy/ , Richard@RichardLeirer.org
