## The home routine (every morning, before anything)

Spinal movement, yoga, abs, cushion. Box breathing 4-4-8-4. Wim Hof breathing then the Rio Hondo when it's running. Granite's walk. This is 60-75 minutes and it is why the rest of the day works.

## Counting

- Meetings: 5-7 a week (AA daily where possible, Recovery Dharma Sunday).
- Temple: 2 fixed (Tue, Sun), plus Fri lunch.
- Body: qi gong 3, Feldenkrais 1, swim 2, ride 2, acupuncture every other week. That's 8-9 sessions, matching the "1-3 hours a day" I already do, but joint-smart.
- Studio: 3 blocks plus a pottery afternoon.
- Service: Shared Table 2x/month, SAR 1x/month, temple kitchen weekly.
- Santa Fe: 1-2 days a month, stacked (men, metal, hot water, dance).
- Work: design and Web3 work fits in Mon/Wed/Fri studio blocks and any morning without snow. If income needs more hours, cut a swim, not a meeting.

## When it gets lonely or dark

The pattern on record: buy a couple of strong beers and isolate. The counter-pattern, in order: call the sponsor, go to the 7 AM meeting, go to the temple kitchen and chop onions for 200 people, walk Granite up Italianos, text Griffin, book Ojo. The calendar is built so that on any given day there is somewhere to be with people who are glad I came.
