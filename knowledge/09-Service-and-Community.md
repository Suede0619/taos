# Service and community in Taos

*Researched 2026-09-28. "Leads with service, then figures out sustainability." The temple is the first commitment ([[02-Hanuman-Temple]]); Acudetox is the professional one ([[15-Acudetox-in-New-Mexico]]). This page is everything else, ranked by fit.*

## Tier 2: good, easy, social

- **Harwood Museum**, 238 Ledoux St: events and gallery-guard roles are quick; docent track needs training. volunteer@harwoodmuseum.org, 575-758-9826 x104.
- **Couse-Sharp Historic Site**, 146 Kit Carson Rd: all docents are volunteers, two-hour tours by appointment. A designer's docent gig. 575-751-0369.
- **Millicent Rogers Museum**: actively recruits volunteers (admissions, education, gardens). 575-758-2462.
- **Taos Center for the Arts**: front-of-house (greeter, concessions), free shows. Anna Forster-Smith, anna@tcataos.org.
- **Habitat for Humanity ReStore**, 16 Hwy 522, El Prado, Tue-Sat 10-5, (575) 776-2165. Store and build-site. Also my materials source for mobiles.
- **Stray Hearts Animal Shelter**, 575-758-2198, https://www.strayhearts.org/volunteer . Apply online, Fear Free course, dog-walker training. Granite would approve.
- **DreamTree Project** (youth shelter), shifts 5-8 AM daily and 6-9 PM Mon-Thu. 575-758-9595.
- **Taos County Senior Program**, 200 Lovato Place, 575-737-8927. At 63 I qualify: meals $1.50 suggested donation, fitness classes. Senior Companion program for 55+ volunteers.
- **Holy Cross Medical Center Auxiliary**, (575) 751-5729. **Anvoi Hospice** serves Taos. End-of-life care is in my psychedelics vault and in my mother's story; hospice volunteering is worth a serious look in year one.
- Field Institute of Taos (based in Arroyo Seco), https://www.fitaos.org/ ; Not Forgotten Outreach veterans farm; Twirl; Taos Winter Sports Team (Kristi Vine, director@taoswintersportsteam.com).

## Taos Ski Valley volunteering

- **Bootpacking volunteer program** (reported Oct 2025): unlimited season pass for 10 days of early-season packing, or a blackout pass for 7 days. Watch skitaos.com in October/November for 2026-27. **If it runs, it may replace the $825 pass.** Physically demanding: bootpacking steep terrain all day. With my spine, do it only if the DEXA is clean.
- Adaptive Sports Program NM runs at Ski Santa Fe, Sandia and Pajarito, not TSV; 4-day volunteer training, intermediate rider required. https://www.adaptivesportsprogram.org/volunteer
