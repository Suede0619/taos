# Kinetic sculpture and mobiles

*Researched 2026-09-28. Taos is thin on metal shops and thick on galleries. Plan: fabricate in bursts in Santa Fe, assemble and balance at home, show in Taos.*

## No Calder-style mobile maker in Taos, which is the opportunity

The local kinetic niche is wind sculpture:
- **Carol Dee's Art Joint** (formerly Envision Gallery), 1405 Paseo del Pueblo Norte, El Prado, on the way to town. Sculpture garden of Lyman Whitaker copper wind sculptures; also Reto Messmer, Hubertus Winnubst. (575) 751-1344, envision@taosnet.com, https://www.caroldeesartjoint.com/ . The owner is a sculptor. **First gallery conversation for hanging mobiles.**
- Mark White Fine Art (Santa Fe) is the other regional kinetic name.

## Where to show

- **The Paseo Project** (Taos's outdoor projection and kinetic art festival) is now biennial: no 2026 festival, **open call for PASEO 2027 artists expected fall 2026**. Kinetic and light work is its core aesthetic. My CryptoLumenati projection-mapping background is directly relevant. https://paseoproject.org/ , paseo@paseoproject.org . **Watch for the call this fall.**
- **TCA Encore Gallery 2027 call for submissions** accepts installation and sculptural work. https://tcataos.org/
- **Taos Fall Arts Festival** (Sept 18 to Oct 4, 2026; 250+ artists). Apply for 2027 via https://taosfallarts.com/
- Taos Arts Council (575-224-2720, info@taosartscouncil.org) links to CaFE calls and the Taos Gallery Association. https://taosartscouncil.org/
- First Saturday gallery walks; TCA First Fridays, free, 5-7 PM.
- The Hanuman temple sunroom and Twirl (free children's discovery space, 225 Camino de la Placita, has a "kinetic solar system") are gift or donation venues. https://www.twirltaos.org/

## Workshop and metal access (the weak spot)

- **MAKE Santa Fe, 2879 All Trades Rd, Santa Fe** (~1 hr 45). Wed-Sun 12-8; **tours any Friday 6 PM**. Individual membership about $105/month; **volunteer 12 hrs/week for free membership**. Required badge classes: Metalshop/MIG, plasma cutter, blacksmithing, ceramics, laser, CNC. https://makesantafe.org/become-a-maker/ . **Plan: take the MIG and plasma badges in December, then do fabrication in monthly Santa Fe bursts** paired with dating, Ten Thousand Waves, and FunG Farms.
- **UNM-Taos:** no welding sections listed for Fall 2026. The old Armory is being renovated as the Taos Workforce Center with welding planned (timing uncertain). **Sculpture I (Wed) and Jewelry & Small Metals I (Fri)** are the current metal-adjacent classes; Small Metals teaches the soldering, wire and sheet skills mobiles actually need. Spring 2027 schedule out in October.
- Welding programs 1-1.5 hr away: Northern New Mexico College, Española (continuing-ed welding lab); Luna Community College, Las Vegas NM (800-588-7232; lists an "ornamental art welding" course).
- Blacksmithing: Santa Fe School of Blacksmithing, Turley Forge, MAKE Santa Fe badge.
- Taos Launchpad makerspace closed pending a new location. The ToolBox (thetoolboxtaos.org) is an online community, no shop. UNM-Taos HIVE (1157 County Rd 110) is coworking, not a shop.
- Taos Art School (575-758-0350, taosartschool.org) lists metal among its media.

## Materials

- **Habitat ReStore, 16 NM-522, El Prado** (nearest) and 206 Paseo del Cañon E, Taos. Tue-Sat 10-5. Copper, brass, aluminum, old fixtures, all cheap.
- No welding-gas or metal retailer in Taos. Stock wire, rod, sheet and tube in Santa Fe or Albuquerque, or order online (McMaster-Carr, OnlineMetals). For mobiles: 1/16" to 1/8" steel or brass rod, aluminum sheet 0.025-0.040", stainless wire, tin snips, a small anvil, pliers, files, and a balance rig. **Most mobile work is hand tools at a kitchen table. Don't wait for a welder.**
- Found materials: elk bones (I painted them during COVID), aspen, river stone from the Rio Hondo, sage. A Taos mobile made of Taos.
