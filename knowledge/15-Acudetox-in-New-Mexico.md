## New Mexico will not simply honor the POCA/NADA card

Verified from 16.2.16 NMAC (amended Oct 2024): https://srca.nm.gov/parts/title16/16.002.0016.html

- NM issues a **Certified Auricular Detoxification Specialist (CADS)** credential through the **Board of Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine** (NM Regulation and Licensing Dept).
- **Scope:** the 5-point NADA protocol and non-penetrating ear devices (seeds, magnets), **only within a board-approved program** focused on substance-use treatment, prevention or harm reduction, **only under a board-registered supervisor** who must be a licensed DOM (max 30 supervisees).
- **Requirements:** HS diploma; 30 hours didactic (NADA-style, clean needle technique, OSHA); **40 supervised client treatments under an NM-registered supervisor**; board clean-needle exam; NM jurisprudence exam (90% pass); affidavit of no drug/alcohol convictions in 2 years.
- **No reciprocity clause** for out-of-state certificates. In practice PHANM treats prior NADA training as satisfying the didactic part, but expect to redo the 40 supervised treatments with an NM supervisor and take the jurisprudence exam. Confirm with the board.
- Fees: application $50, annual renewal $30 (expires July 31), late fee $20.
- A proposed rule change to 16.2.16 was posted October 2025: https://www.rld.nm.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/AOM-Proposed-Rule-Change-16.2.16-NMAC-CADs-1.pdf . Check whether it was adopted; it may loosen or tighten the above.
- **The affidavit:** "no drug/alcohol convictions in 2 years." My history includes DUIs; the most recent is on the 2020 treatment plan. Confirm dates before applying. If a conviction falls inside the window, wait it out.
