## Riding Taos on a board, with this body

Mountain: 1,294 acres, 110 trails, base 9,207 ft, summit 12,481 ft, 3,281 ft vertical, about 300 inches average, 60% north-facing. Roughly 24% beginner, 25% intermediate, 51% expert. Snowboards have been welcome since 2008.

**Where to ride**
- **Lift 4 (back side high-speed quad) is home base:** Honeysuckle, Shalako, Patton, Bambi, Lower Hunziker Bowl. Long, groomed, sunny, forgiving.
- **Front-side warmups:** Powderhorn, White Feather, Lower Stauffenberg. Maxie's terrain park if the knees are brave.
- **Steeps for when the body says yes:** Al's Run, Spencer's, Werner's, Longhorn off Lift 2; hike-to Highline Ridge and West Basin (10-15 min bootpack); Kachina Peak and Hunziker Bowl.
- **Flats to keep speed on:** the Rubezahl catwalk home (closes about 4:15), traverses off the top of Lift 4, the Kachina runout. Unstrap early or carry speed; this is the snowboarder's tax at Taos.
- Guide: https://opensnow.com/news/post/insider-s-guide-to-skiing-taos-ski-valley-new-mexico-where-aspirations-thrive

**Protecting the spine, hip and knee** (see [[01-Me-in-Taos]]: two compression fractures, right knee OA, right hip half-gone)
- Armor: helmet, **back protector**, **impact shorts**, wrist guards. Non-negotiable.
- First two weeks: groomers only, half days, quit while fresh. Falls happen when tired.
- Ride weekdays (that's the pass), avoid holiday crowds, avoid icy mornings after a thaw. Late-morning starts on cold days suit both the body and the Weekday pass.
- Get the DEXA result before opening day. If it says osteopenia or worse, talk to the doctor about whether Kachina and moguls are off the table this year.
- Stretch and qi gong on ride days; cold plunge after.
- Consider one **snowboard clinic** early to reset technique for older joints (Ernie Blake Snowsports School, 575-776-5918, 15% off with pass). Early Season Ski Weeks Dec 13-19 and Jan 3-9 at $220 are ski-focused, but the school does daily adult snowboard classes.
