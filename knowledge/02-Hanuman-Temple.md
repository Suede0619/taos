# Neem Karoli Baba Ashram and Hanuman Temple, Taos

*Researched 2026-09-28 from nkbashram.org, newsletters and related sites. "Confirmed" means posted on the ashram site as of today; "estimated" means built from past years or the Hindu calendar.*

**Why this is the center of the plan:** Maharaj-ji and Amma are on my altar. Kirtan is my reliable source of joy. And the flying Hanuman murti, carved in Jaipur in 1977-78, was first housed **in Arroyo Seco** by a New Mexico devotee. The first bhandara was held there in September 1978 before the murti moved to the Taos farm that became the ashram. I am moving to the village where Hanuman first landed in America.

## Seva: how I get in

The seva page welcomes locals directly: check the daily seva board, talk to the on-duty caretaker, or email the manager. **"If you'd like to commit to a weekly seva role, feel free to reach out."** No application for day volunteers. Given the staffing shortage, a reliable weekly local is gold.

Seva areas:
- **Maharaj-ji's Kitchen:** cooking Tuesday dinner and weekend lunches for 200-300 people a day, dishes, canning. Protocol: shower and clean clothes; no tasting before offering; no eating while preparing; minimal talk. This is my first choice: it's the heart of the place and it's where friendships form.
- **Farm and grounds:** 10 acres, orchard, two greenhouses, medicinal herb garden, peafowl, 400-year-old acequia. Winter needs: greenhouse, wood stoves, chimneys, maintenance.
- **Skills they list:** carpentry, natural building, maintenance, **music and fine arts**, retail (Puja Dukan), office, **technology support**, cleaning. Tech and design support is an obvious fit for me; a kinetic mobile for the sunroom is a possible offering later.
- **Construction:** the $850K Prasad Dining Hall awaits permits expected fall 2026. Construction seva may open during my stay.
- **Festival prep:** heavy need before Shivaratri (March) and Hanuman Jayanti (April).

**Live-in programs, for reference:** Winter Sevaks (Nov-Mar, 6-8 weeks, housing and meals) and full-time caretakers are advertised, though the buttons currently say "not accepting applications." Not needed since I have housing, but worth knowing if the Suzie arrangement ever fails.
