# Dating and queer life: Taos, Santa Fe, Albuquerque

*Researched 2026-09-28. The honest frame: Taos County has ~35,000 people, so the pool here is thin with a ski-season bump. Santa Fe (75 min) has one of the highest same-sex-household rates in the US and a large retired gay male population but no full-time gay bar since 2015, which suits a sober man. Albuquerque (2.5 hr) has the numbers and the men's outdoor groups. The plan is a two-town life: Taos for practice and craft, Santa Fe one or two days a month for men.*

## What I said I want

Two two-year relationships, in love once. I find the gay scene heavy on drinking, materialism and shallowness. I'm attracted to bright, charming, handsome men. Therapy goal: stop giving off "go away" vibes; say what I want before I'm refused. The pattern to break is waiting to be chosen. The Taos version of dating is to be visibly, warmly present in rooms where men who share my values already are, and to ask.

## Taos

- **Taos Pride** (https://taospride.org, Instagram @taospride). Pride on the Plaza is in August; film fest in June; both after this lease's first winter. **DM them in November and ask about winter planning meetings and the "council of queer elders"** the Taos News wrote about ("We're here. We're queer.", taosnews.com). That council is the network.
- **Taos Community Foundation LGBTQ+ Equity Fund**, 115 La Posta Rd Ste A, 575-737-9300, info@taoscf.org. Seeded Taos Pride. A first meeting for anyone who wants to give or serve; ask who's doing queer work locally.
- **Queer-owned and queer-welcoming** (EDGE, Aug 2026, "Why Taos is quietly becoming the queer desert escape"): Casa Gallina (gay-owned inn), Jones Walker of Taos (gay-owned gallery), Sugar Nymphs Bistro (lesbian-owned, Peñasco), a queer-owned micro-bakery with community baking workshops (name to find), a cluster of queer-owned galleries and cafes.
- No confirmed monthly LGBTQ social in Taos. No PFLAG. No queer ski group. **Which means starting one is the move:** a monthly queer coffee at Sol Food or a first-Friday gallery walk group. "In, Out, & About" (below) explicitly encourages members to host in their own towns.
- **Ecstatic Dance Taos** (Sundays ~10-12) and the **Hanuman temple** Tuesday kirtan are the sober rooms where the seekers are. Queer men are reliably present in both.
- Unitarian Congregation of Taos (uutaos.org) is liberal and inclusive.

No cover, hosted appetizers, often hundreds of men, **skews 45+**. Lobby-mixer format, alcohol present but not the point. ## Albuquerque

- **Gay Out West NM** (https://www.meetup.com/gay-out-west-nm/): 1,013 members, gay/bi/trans men, light hikes, hot springs, camping. **Winter Solstice sunset hike Dec 21.**
- **In, Out, & About** (https://meetup.com/In-Out-About-A-New-Mexico-Gay-Social-Group): 418 members, gay men's social group founded 2013, explicitly "outside bars or dating apps," statewide, **encourages members to host in their own towns.** Host a Taos snowshoe or a temple Tuesday.
- **Gay Outdoors New Mexico** Meetup (1-2 events/month).
- **ORCA (Older Rainbow Community Albuquerque)**, https://orcaabq.org; monthly meeting 2nd Thursday 2-4 PM, Highland Senior Center, 131 Monroe NE.
- Effex Nightclub, 420 Central Ave SW, if a dance floor is ever the point.
- Albuquerque Pride June 11-13, 2027 (verify).
- New Mexico Gay Men's Chorus is Albuquerque-based.

## Apps, set for a rural man

- **Scruff**: best fit, skews 35+, distance-tolerant. Use Venture to set location to Santa Fe on Thursdays before a weekend trip.
- **Grindr**: highest raw density in Santa Fe and ABQ; use Explore. Expect little within 15 miles of Seco except ski-season visitors.
- **Hinge**: 100-mile radius covers Santa Fe. Prompts suit a designer with a spiritual life. Say "sober" plainly.
- **OkCupid**: still the best values-matching for the Santa Fe demographic (spiritual, outdoorsy, progressive).
- SilverDaddies / Daddyhunt: niche but active among 50+ men in NM. Match has some Santa Fe men; SilverSingles very few.
- Profile: photos of me actually doing things (board, temple, mobile, Granite). In a region this small, everyone recognizes everyone. Be kind on the way out of every conversation.

## The house rules and dating

Overnight guests allowed with a heads-up, two nights max. Ask Suzie how she'd like the heads-up (text the day before is a fair default). Santa Fe nights can also be Santa Fe nights: the temple's two guest rooms are for Friday and Saturday, and a cheap Santa Fe room removes the drive-home-at-midnight problem.

## The rhythm

- Monthly: Friends of Dorothy (Friday), HRA After 5 (3rd Thursday), LGBTQ+ 50+ coffee.
- Weekly: ecstatic dance, temple Tuesday.
- Quarterly: an ABQ men's hike or host one in Taos.
- Daily: five minutes on Scruff/Hinge, not fifty.
