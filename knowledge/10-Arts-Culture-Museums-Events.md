# Arts, culture, museums, and the winter events calendar

*Researched 2026-09-28. Taos has more museums per capita than almost any town its size. Taos County residents get in free on Sundays at the Harwood and the Fechin house, and free always at the Kit Carson House. Get the NM license first, then museum-hop on Sundays after Chalisas.*

## Taos Pueblo winter ceremonies (public, no photography)

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Dec 24 | Procession of the Virgin with bonfires, Mass about 5 PM |
| Dec 25 | Deer Dance or Matachines (1 PM or 11 AM) |
| Jan 1 | Turtle Dance, transfer of canes |
| Jan 6 | Deer and Buffalo dances (Three Kings Day) |
| May 3 | Santa Cruz feast day corn dance and foot races |

Go as a guest. Dress warm, stand back, no photos, no questions during the dances. This is the oldest continuously inhabited community in the country and my new neighbor.

## Performing arts

- **Taos Center for the Arts (TCA)**, 133 Paseo del Pueblo Norte, (575) 758-2052. Membership $55. First Fridays free 5-7 PM. Volunteer front-of-house gets you into shows free. **Taos Film Festival (2nd annual) Apr 29 to May 2, 2027.** https://tcataos.org/
- **Taos Chamber Music Group** at the Harwood, 575-224-2106, https://taoschambermusicgroup.org/ :
  - Nov 7-8 Bach Musical Offering
  - Dec 11 Gleb Ivanov piano recital
  - Dec 12-13 All-Franck
  - Feb 13-14 Romantic Valentine
  - Mar 13-14 Cello Extravaganza
  - Apr 10-11 Taos School of Music Alumni
  - May 22-23 Rising Stars
- **Live music, no cover:** Adobe Bar at the Taos Inn (nightly), Sagebrush Inn cantina (nightly, big dance floor). Taos Mesa Brewing Mothership, 20 ABC Mesa Rd, El Prado (touring acts: Thievery Corporation, California Honeydrops upcoming). Old Martinez Hall, 4140 NM-68, reopened (Fri/Sat 3-8, Sun 11-8). KTAOS Solar Center is reportedly closed; KTAOS 101.9 concerts now at Mary's Place @ Taos Lifestyle.
- **Arroyo Seco Live**, the Seco stage: free world-music concerts mostly summer; winter has Sunday donation yoga and monthly craft events at Wilde.Ink. https://www.secolive.org/
- **Abe's Cantina y Cocina**, 489 NM-150 (since 1945), occasional live music, my corner bar in the non-drinking sense: breakfast burritos.

## Literary and learning

- **SOMOS**, 108 Civic Plaza Dr, 575-758-0081: **eight-week Winter Writers Series Jan-Feb 2027**; open mics year-round. https://somostaos.org/calendar/ . I wrote during COVID; a winter writing series in a mountain town is a classic for a reason.
- **Taos Public Library**, 402 Camino de la Placita: Spanish conversation club Saturdays 11 AM, beginner Spanish Thursdays, yoga and knitting via Friends of the Library; Friends' ski-movie fundraiser at TCA in early December. https://friendsoftaoslibrary.org/
- **UNM-Taos**: spring 2027 credit classes (ceramics, sculpture, small metals, drawing). Community Ed non-credit: call 575-737-6215. https://taos.unm.edu/academics/classes/class-schedule.html
- **Earthship Biotecture**, Tres Piedras: visitor center daily 9-4 ($9 self-guided, $22 guided at 4 PM). Field Study Session Nov 9-27, 2026 and **Mar 8-26, 2027**. 575-751-0462. https://earthship.com/schedule/ . Solarpunk in adobe and tires, 30 minutes away.
- SMU-in-Taos lectures are summer-only.
