# Outdoors and adventures, November to May

*Researched 2026-09-28. Arroyo Seco is at ~7,600 ft, on the road between the Rio Grande Gorge (20 min west) and Taos Ski Valley (25 min east). Granite comes on everything except the temple and the ski hill.*

## Right out the door: the Hwy 150 canyons

Four trailheads between the house and the ski valley, all starting above 8,000 ft, creek crossings, avalanche terrain higher up. Snowshoe or microspike in winter, hike in spring.

| Trail | One way | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gavilan | 3.3 mi | steep, aspens |
| Italianos | 3.7 mi | creek canyon |
| Yerba Canyon | 3.9 mi | to Lobo Peak ridge in summer |
| Manzanita | 4.2 mi | |
| Bull of the Woods #90 | 3 mi | from the TSV base lot, to the meadow and Wheeler Peak trail |
| Williams Lake | 4 mi round trip | from the Bavarian (4x4 road); the classic winter snowshoe |

- **Taos Avalanche Center**: forecasts December to early April, occasional classes. https://www.taosavalanchecenter.org . Take the class in December. I know snow from climbing; refresh it.
- Rentals: Taos Mountain Outfitters 575-758-9292, Mudd N Flood 575-751-9100, Cottam's at TSV 575-776-8719. Buy microspikes and snowshoes; rent nothing twice.

## Low-elevation winter hikes (dry, sunny, dog-friendly)

- **Devisadero Loop** (east edge of town, 5 mi), **West Rim Trail** along the Rio Grande Gorge from the Gorge Bridge, **Rio Grande Gorge at Taos Junction Bridge** (Orilla Verde), **Wild Rivers** (Questa, La Junta and Big Arsenic trails down to the river), **Rift Valley / Vista Verde** near Pilar. All under 7,500 ft, mostly snow-free by afternoon.
- **John Dunn Bridge, Arroyo Hondo**, 10 minutes away: the gorge floor, Black Rock hot springs, and the confluence of the Rio Hondo (my creek) with the Rio Grande.

## Nordic

Enchanted Forest is closed for 2026-27. Options: **Amole Canyon** (Carson NF #10, Hwy 518, 20 mi south, blue-diamond loops 1-6+ mi, ~8,200 ft, Taos Nordic Ski Club sets track), the 150 canyons, Valles Caldera (2 hr), Ski Santa Fe's Norski trails.

## Uphill at the ski valley

Conflicting reports: the prior-season policy allowed uphill from the Lift 1 base 7-9 AM with a pass and waiver, but one 2026 summary said skinning is not allowed. **Verify with TSV before touring.** The new patrol-guided Full Moon Uphill Tours (Dec 26, Jan 23, Feb 20, Mar 20) are the sanctioned version.

## Hot springs

Ojo Caliente (weekday soak $45), Black Rock (free, 10 min, call BLM 575-758-8851 about the road), Manby (river access only). Details in [[04-Body-Energy-Qigong-TCM]].

## Climbing

I was a high-level rock, ice and aid climber. The knee and spine say "moderate," not "never."
- **Dead Cholla Wall** and the Rio Grande Gorge basalt near Pilar and the John Dunn Bridge: sport and trad, sunny in winter.
- **Tres Piedras**: granite domes 30 min northwest, slab and crack.
- Ice: Taos-area ice is thin and fickle; the closer reliable ice is in Colorado. Let it go this year.
- Guides and partners: Mountain Skills (575-776-2222), Taos Rock Gym if open. Ask at SAR meetings for partners.
- A top-rope day at Tres Piedras with Griffin in April is a good goal.

## Spring: rivers and bikes

- **Rio Grande rafting**: the Taos Box runs April-June when flow exceeds 600-700 cfs. 2026 was low; call outfitters in March (New Mexico River Adventures, Los Rios, Far Flung). A Racecourse half-day in late April is the season opener.
- **Mountain biking** (spring, when dry): Rift Valley near Pilar, West Rim, Talpa Traverse, and in May the lower South Boundary. Rent from Mudd N Flood or Gearing Up.
- Wheeler Peak (13,161 ft) is a June-plus objective; Williams Lake to the summit in early summer, with the hip's permission.

## Day trips

Bandelier (2 hr), Ghost Ranch (1.5 hr; NM Men's Wellness holds retreats there), Valles Caldera (2 hr), Great Sand Dunes (2.5 hr), Chama and the Cumbres & Toltec (summer), Abiquiu and the Chama River, Ojo Caliente, Santa Fe.

## Granite at altitude and in cold

Weimaraners are thin-coated. A coat for sub-20°F mornings, paw balm for ice, booties on the road salt, and no long snowshoe days until he's acclimated. Water on every hike; the air is dry. Vet: find one in the first month (Taos has several; ask Suzie). Coyotes, and in Seco the occasional mountain lion: leash at dusk.
